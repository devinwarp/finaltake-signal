---
name: Claim status colors
description: Semantic visual distinction between demo verification and owner attestation.
---

Owner-approved custom claims must not inherit the green verification treatment of brand-verified example claims. Use separate, legible styling while retaining the explicit approval/disclosure text.

**Why:** Owner attestation is not independent verification by FinalTake. A shared green status would imply an assurance the product does not provide.

**How to apply:** When extending claim controls or adding new claim surfaces, distinguish brand-verified demo language, owner-approved custom language, unverified/disabled items, and guardrails without relying on color alone.
